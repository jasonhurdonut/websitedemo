Our Mission

Established Spring 2022 — Charles H. Dyson School
As Dyson is ever-growing and ever-evolving, this team of students was established with the express purpose of advising the Dean and other school leaders on what matters most to undergraduates.
i.

Listen broadly.

Engage Dyson undergraduates from different backgrounds and experiences so every voice and opinion is heard at the table.

ii.

Bridge the gap.

Represent and communicate with key Dyson student organizations and act as the connection between the student body and the Dean's office.

iii.

Drive real change.

Initiate and support proposals, plans, and actions to improve the holistic Dyson student experience.
